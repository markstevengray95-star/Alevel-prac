SKILLS & ANALYSIS

Practical toolkit

Useful checks for planning, processing and evaluating experiments.

Uncertainty

Record instrument resolution, estimate absolute uncertainty, convert to percentage uncertainty and propagate appropriately.

percentage uncertainty = (absolute uncertainty ÷ measured value) × 100%

Graphs

Put the independent variable on the x-axis, include units, use a sensible scale, plot accurately and use a best-fit line or curve.

gradient = Δy / Δx

Evaluation

Link improvements to a named source of uncertainty or systematic error. Explain how each change improves the quality of the evidence.

claim → evidence → limitation → improvement

Repeatability

Repeat measurements across the full range, identify anomalous values carefully and calculate means where appropriate.

mean = Σ readings / number of readings

Linearisation

If the relationship is not linear, rearrange the model into a straight-line form and identify what the gradient and intercept represent.

y = mx + c

Risk thinking

Identify the hazard, who could be harmed, the control measure and any remaining risk. Keep controls specific to the apparatus and method.

hazard → risk → control
