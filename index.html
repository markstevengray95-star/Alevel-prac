PRACTICAL SKILLS

Analysis toolkit

Uncertainty

Record resolution, repeats, range and percentage uncertainty. The workbench shows how measurement precision affects the final result.

% uncertainty = Δx / x × 100

Linearisation

Choose transformed axes that turn the model into a straight line: for example T² against L, V against I or ln(V) against t.

y = mx + c

Best-fit thinking

Compare the fitted gradient with the theoretical relationship, then distinguish random scatter from systematic offset.

R² closer to 1 → stronger linear fit

Evaluation

Link every improvement to a specific limitation: parallax, timing reaction, heating, alignment, background counts or instrument resolution.

limitation → effect → improvement
